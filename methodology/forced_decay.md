# Forced-decay methodology

The bbbar gammagamma topology is rare. The analysis therefore uses a controlled forced-decay construction to oversample this topology while preserving the generated Higgs four-momenta.

The decay construction uses isotropic two-body Higgs decays. Validation checks include four-vector conservation, Higgs-mass preservation, two-body kinematics, and decay-angle behaviour.

The forced-decay procedure is used as an event-sampling technique for the phenomenological study. Its validation does not establish exact equivalence to every possible detector-level observable of a naturally decayed sample.
