# Detector selection

The baseline detector selection is deliberately simplified and is intended for phenomenological reconstruction studies.

Jets:
pT > 25 GeV and |eta| < 2.5.

Photons:
pT > 25 GeV and |eta| < 2.5.

At least two jets, at least two b-tagged jets, and at least two photons are required.

The b-tagged jet pair whose invariant mass is closest to 125 GeV is used as the bb Higgs candidate. The two leading photons form the diphoton candidate.

Final Higgs-mass requirements:

100 < m_bb < 150 GeV

120 < m_gammagamma < 130 GeV

This should not be interpreted as a reproduction of the complete ATLAS analysis selection, trigger, background categorisation, or systematic model.
