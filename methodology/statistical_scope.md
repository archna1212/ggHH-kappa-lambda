# Statistical scope

The present study is a detector-level phenomenology baseline.

It does not include:

- a full bbbar gammagamma background model;
- detector/systematic nuisance parameters;
- a full experimental likelihood;
- collision data;
- the complete ATLAS statistical model.

The multivariate study compares signal hypotheses and is explicitly signal-vs-signal.

Any signal-only rate-plus-shape statistical quantities are descriptive/illustrative and are not experimental significance or exclusion limits.
